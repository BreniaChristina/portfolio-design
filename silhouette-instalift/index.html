What Can I Expect From the Silhouette InstaLift®?

The Silhouette InstaLift® lifts saggy skin in the mid-face by using specialized threads inserted under the skin in the cheeks and jaws. This thread has tiny cones that act as scaffolding, tightening, smoothing, and volumizing the subdermal tissue of the skin.

Patients should expect immediate results that last up to two years. Some patients may experience minor bruising and minimal swelling after the procedure but should be able to return to work the next day.

After these specialized threads are inserted under the skin, the small cones encourage the body's natural collagen production. Lasting up to about two years, the Silhouette InstaLift® will dissolve. However, the collagen stimulated by these threads continues to help the tissue stay strong, resilient, and withstand stretching, even after the threads are gone.

Facts about Silhouette InstaLift®

We want to share a few other facts with you about this treatment.

The Silhouette InstaLift® is:

  • FDA Approved
  • Done under local anesthetic
  • Takes only about 45-minutes to do
  • Threads are biodegradable and dissolve away naturally under the skin of the patient
  • Threads are tiny and flexible. After healing, patients should not notice they are there in their day to day lives
  • After dissolving into the patient's body, more Silhouette InstaLift® threads can be inserted
